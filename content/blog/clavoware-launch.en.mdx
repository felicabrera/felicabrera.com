Today [Clavoware](https://clavoware.com) is out in early access.

## The problem

In a supermarket, stock expires quietly on the shelf. Nobody checks dates product by product, and whatever is found too late ends up in the trash. It is not a problem of careless people: it is a problem of information arriving late.

## How it works

1. **When stock arrives**, staff scan the barcode with their phone camera and enter the expiry date in three taps. It works offline: scans are queued and sync when the connection returns.
2. **Every day at 08:00**, the manager gets a summary: what expires within the next 7 days, what is critical within 3, and what to do with each item: discount, move or remove it.
3. **Chains compare their stores** by waste, to see where the most is lost and why.

No dedicated hardware needed: it runs on Android and iOS, in Spanish, Portuguese and English.

## Under the hood

- A **Go API** built with Fiber and PostgreSQL.
- An offline-first **Next.js web app** with camera scanning (ZXing), an offline scan queue and push notifications.
- Docker to deploy and Sentry to hear about errors before users do.

## Early access

We are opening Clavoware to supermarkets in Uruguay and the region. If you run or manage one and want to try it, head to [clavoware.com](https://clavoware.com).
